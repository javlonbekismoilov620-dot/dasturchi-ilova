# Graphic design curriculum

**Goal:** take a learner with no design background to a professional designer who can create brand identities, layouts and digital product interfaces, justify decisions, run a client or product process, and present a strong portfolio.

**Track overview**

```text
Zero          Design Principles from Zero (exists, expand)
Beginner      Colour & Typography → Figma Essentials · Photoshop Essentials · Vector Design (Illustrator)
Intermediate  Logo Design & Brand Identity · UI Design · UX Design Fundamentals
Advanced      Design Systems · Digital Illustration · Print & Production · Motion for Designers
Professional  Product Design & Portfolio (capstone)
Expert        Specialisations: brand strategy · design leadership · type design
```

**Tool policy:**
- **Figma** (free tier) is the primary tool for layout and UI.
- **Photoshop** and **Illustrator** are taught because employers ask for them. Each course also names free or cheaper alternatives (Photopea, Affinity, Inkscape) and explains the licence costs up front.

**Local relevance.** Typography lessons cover bilingual and two-script design (Latin Uzbek with oʻ/gʻ and ʼ, and Cyrillic), and how to choose fonts that support both.

Total guided learning is about 300–340 hours, plus projects.

**Level:** Zero · **Access:** free · **Status:** published, 3 of 10 lessons · **About 10 h**

## G-1 · Colour & Typography

**Level:** Beginner · **Access:** premium · **About 22 h** · **Prerequisite:** G-0

**Outcomes:** learners will be able to build accessible colour systems, set readable typography in two scripts, and choose and pair typefaces with confidence.

| Module | Lessons | Practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Colour theory | Colour models (RGB, CMYK, HEX, HSL) · Harmony and palettes · Colour and culture | Palette from a photograph |
| 2. Accessible colour | Contrast ratios (WCAG) · Colour blindness · Never relying on colour alone | Fix an inaccessible interface |
| 3. Type anatomy | Parts of letters · Classifications · Weights and styles | Identify typefaces |
| 4. Setting text | Line length, leading and tracking · Hierarchy with type · Alignment | Article layout |
| 5. Pairing | Pairing principles · Variable fonts · Licences (free vs commercial, web vs print) | Three pairings with reasons |
| 6. Two-script typography | Latin Uzbek characters and the ʻ/ʼ marks · Cyrillic support · Bilingual layouts | Bilingual flyer |
| Final | Project: *Bilingual event poster and social post* · Final assessment | |

## G-2 · Figma Essentials

**Level:** Beginner · **About 20 h** · **Prerequisite:** G-0

Modules:
1. Frames, shapes and layers
2. Auto layout
3. Constraints and responsive frames
4. Components and variants
5. Styles and variables
6. Prototyping and interactions
7. Collaboration and comments
8. Exporting assets
9. Dev mode and handoff basics

**Project:** a five-screen mobile app mock-up with components.

## G-3 · Photoshop Essentials

**Level:** Beginner · **About 20 h** · **Prerequisite:** G-0

Modules:
1. Raster vs vector, and resolution
2. Layers and masks
3. Selections, including AI-assisted selection, used responsibly
4. Adjustment layers
5. Retouching ethics and techniques
6. Compositing
7. Text and smart objects
8. Exporting for web and print

**Project:** a social campaign visual set (3 formats).

## G-4 · Vector Design (Illustrator)

**Level:** Beginner → Intermediate · **About 20 h** · **Prerequisite:** G-0

Modules:
1. Vectors and paths
2. The pen tool, with drills
3. Shape building and pathfinder
4. Strokes, fills and gradients
5. Type on paths and outlines
6. Grids and alignment for icons
7. Exporting SVG

**Project:** an icon set of 12 consistent icons. Steps for Inkscape are given alongside.

## G-5 · Logo Design & Brand Identity

**Level:** Intermediate · **About 28 h** · **Prerequisites:** G-1, G-4

Modules:
1. What a brand is (beyond the logo)
2. Briefs and research
3. Sketching and concept development
4. Logo construction: geometry, optical adjustments
5. Wordmarks for Latin and Cyrillic names
6. Colour and type systems for brands
7. Imagery and tone of voice
8. Brand guidelines
9. Presenting to clients

**Project:** a complete identity for a local business: logo, palette, typography, applications and a guidelines PDF.

## G-6 · UI Design

**Level:** Intermediate · **About 28 h** · **Prerequisites:** G-1, G-2

Modules:
1. Interface anatomy
2. Spacing systems and grids
3. Components and states (hover, focus, disabled, error)
4. Forms that work
5. Data display
6. Accessibility (WCAG) in UI
7. Platform conventions (Apple HIG, Material Design)
8. Dark mode
9. Handoff and working with developers

**Project:** a complete app UI (12+ screens) with states and a component sheet.
