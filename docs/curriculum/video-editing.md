# Video editing & production curriculum

**Goal:** take a learner who has only watched videos to a professional editor who can shoot for the edit, cut a compelling story, mix sound, grade colour, add motion graphics and deliver to client and platform specifications.

**Track overview**

```text
Zero          Video Editing Fundamentals (exists, expand)
Beginner      DaVinci Resolve Essentials (in review) · Shooting for the Edit
              (optional tool courses: Premiere Pro Essentials · CapCut for Social Video)
Intermediate  Storytelling & Cinematic Editing · Sound for Video · Colour Correction & Grading
Advanced      Motion Graphics & Titles · Social & Commercial Video · Documentary Filmmaking
Professional  Professional Post-Production Workflow & Showreel (capstone)
Expert        Specialisations: colourist · sound designer · motion designer · director/editor
```

**Tool policy.** The core track uses **DaVinci Resolve**, which has a capable free version and runs on Windows, macOS and Linux. Premiere Pro (paid) and CapCut (mobile and social) get shorter, tool-specific courses that reuse the same concepts. Every course states the tool version.

**Footage.** Mahorat provides licensed practice footage and music with every hands-on course, so learners don't need a camera to start. Each course states clearly what the learner may do with that footage.

Total guided learning is about 280–320 hours, plus projects.

## V-1b · Premiere Pro Essentials *(optional)* and V-1c · CapCut for Social Video *(optional)*

Each is about 12–14 h and covers the same concepts as V-1, mapped to that tool:
- **Premiere Pro:** projects and bins, editing and trimming, Essential Sound, Lumetri basics, exporting.
- **CapCut:** vertical editing, captions, templates (and when not to use them), exporting for each platform.

## V-2 · Shooting for the Edit

**Level:** Beginner · **About 20 h** · **Prerequisite:** V-0

Modules:
1. Cameras and phones: what matters
2. Exposure: aperture, shutter (the 180° rule) and ISO
3. Frame rates and slow motion
4. White balance
5. Composition and movement
6. Coverage: master, singles, inserts and B-roll
7. Recording clean audio
8. Lighting with practical and cheap lights
9. Shot lists and planning

**Project:** shoot and edit a 90-second scene with full coverage. A phone is enough.

## V-3 · Storytelling & Cinematic Editing

**Level:** Intermediate · **About 24 h** · **Prerequisite:** V-1

Modules:
1. Story structure for short and long form
2. Pacing and rhythm
3. Continuity editing and when to break it
4. Match cuts and graphic matches
5. Montage
6. Performance selection
7. Emotion through cutting
8. Using music and silence
9. Feedback and versioning

**Project:** re-edit the same footage three times for three tones (comedy, drama, advert), with written intentions.

## V-4 · Sound for Video

**Level:** Intermediate · **About 22 h** · **Prerequisite:** V-1

Modules:
1. How sound works
2. Dialogue editing and cleanup
3. EQ, compression and de-essing
4. Room tone and ambience
5. Sound effects and Foley basics
6. Music editing to picture
7. Loudness standards (streaming targets around −14 LUFS integrated, broadcast EBU R128 at −23 LUFS) and metering
8. Stems and delivery

**Project:** a complete mix of a provided scene, delivered to two loudness targets.

## V-5 · Colour Correction & Grading

**Level:** Intermediate → Advanced · **About 26 h** · **Prerequisite:** V-1

Modules:
1. How colour and light work for video
2. Reading scopes: waveform, parade, vectorscope
3. Balancing exposure and white balance
4. Node-based workflow in Resolve
5. Log footage, colour spaces (Rec.709) and LUTs used correctly
6. Shot matching
7. Secondary corrections: qualifiers, windows, tracking
8. Skin tones
9. Creating looks
10. Grading for different platforms

**Project:** grade a 2-minute sequence (balance, match, look) with before/after stills.

## V-6 · Motion Graphics & Titles

**Level:** Advanced · **About 26 h** · **Prerequisite:** V-1 (design course G-0 recommended)

Modules:
1. Motion design principles: easing, timing, hierarchy in motion
2. Keyframes and graph editing
3. Text animation and lower thirds
4. Shape layers and simple illustration
5. Tracking and stabilising
6. Templates for editors
7. Tools: After Effects or Resolve Fusion, with parallel instructions

**Project:** an animated title sequence and a lower-third template pack.

## V-7 · Social & Commercial Video

**Level:** Advanced · **About 20 h** · **Prerequisites:** V-3, V-4

Modules:
1. Briefs and objectives
2. Hooks and the first three seconds
3. Vertical framing and safe zones
4. Captions and accessibility
5. Platform specifications (checked at recording time)
6. Advert structures
7. Cutdowns and versioning
8. Music licensing
9. Working with brands

**Project:** a 30-second advert plus three platform cutdowns from one shoot.

## V-8 · Documentary Filmmaking

**Level:** Advanced · **About 24 h** · **Prerequisites:** V-2, V-3

Modules:
1. Finding and researching stories
2. Interview technique
3. Ethics, consent and release forms
4. Shooting observational footage
5. Structuring with paper edits
6. Archive and rights
7. Narration and music

## V-P · Professional Post-Production Workflow & Showreel (capstone)

**Level:** Professional · **About 30 h** · **Prerequisites:** V-3, V-4, V-5

Modules:
1. Codecs and containers (H.264, H.265, ProRes, DNxHR) and when to use each
2. Proxies and conform
3. Collaboration and shared storage
4. Review and approval tools
5. Versioning and naming
6. Delivery specifications and QC
7. Archiving
8. Pricing, contracts and client communication
9. Building a showreel and portfolio

**Capstone:** a complete client-style project from brief to delivered masters, plus a 60-second showreel. A mentor reviews it.
